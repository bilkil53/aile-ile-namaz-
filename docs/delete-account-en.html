Account and Data Deletion

You can have your account and data in the Aile İle Namaz app (developer: bilkil53) deleted in two ways.

1. From the app (immediately)

  1. Open the app and tap ⚙ Settings at the top right.
  2. In the Other section, tap Delete my account and data.
  3. Confirm twice. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

2. If you cannot access the app

Create an account deletion request. Writing the name shown in the app is enough; do not share your email there. Or send an email titled "Account deletion request" from your Google account's email address to e.bilkil5391@gmail.com. You will be contacted through your request to verify your identity. The request is completed within 30 days at the latest.

Data that is deleted

  • Your Google account link, your name and email
  • Your prayer records and kaza counts
  • Your family circle memberships, titles, dua and notification records
  • Your notification ID (device address) and notification preferences

In family circles where you are the head of family and that have other members, the head role passes to the oldest member; other people's data in the circle are not deleted. No personal data are kept after deletion; only the infrastructure provider's short-term technical security records are deleted when their own period ends.
