Privacy Policy and KVKK Information Notice

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · App: Aile İle Namaz (com.bilkil53.aile_ile_namaz)

This notice is provided under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (the "Law") and the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles for Fulfilling the Obligation to Inform. It explains which personal data the Aile İle Namaz app processes, for what purposes and on what legal grounds, to whom the data are transferred, and what your rights are. This notice is for information only; it does not ask for your approval of this notice. Your explicit consent for your special categories of personal data is obtained separately, through the Explicit Consent Text below.

1. Data controller

Data controller: the developer of the Aile İle Namaz app (GitHub: bilkil53). Contact: e.bilkil5391@gmail.com.

2. Personal data processed, purposes and legal grounds

  • Name and email address from your Google account (we never access your password). Purpose: to create your account, let you sign in and identify you. Your email is not shown to other users. Legal ground: establishment and performance of a contract (Law Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Prayer marks, kaza counts, dua and greeting messages; family circle memberships, titles and icons. Purpose: to provide the app's core service (prayer tracking, family circle). This information is shown only to people in the same family circle; which prayer time your kaza prayers belong to is visible only to you. Because prayer marks, kaza counts, dua and greeting messages may reveal your religious belief, they are special categories of personal data under Article 6 of the Law and are processed only on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 6(3)(a)). Family circle memberships, titles and icons are processed for the performance of a contract (Art. 5(2)(c)). If you do not give explicit consent, you cannot sign in; you can still view the app in browsing mode.
  • Notification ID (device address), app language and your notification preferences (which notifications you want to receive, whether they show on the lock screen). Purpose: to deliver family notifications as you prefer and in the right language, even when the app is closed. Legal ground: performance of a contract (Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Error report: app version, Android version, memory use, whether the app closed, the error text and the code lines where the error occurred. Sent only if you choose "Send report" in the error window; nothing is sent automatically. The report record does not contain any identifier of your account. Before sending, email addresses, ID numbers, phone numbers, session keys, row values in database error details and file paths in the text are removed automatically; because this cleaning works on specific patterns, it may not catch every piece of information in the error text. Purpose: to fix app errors. Legal ground: legitimate interest (Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Technical records kept by the infrastructure provider during sign-in and connections (IP address, time). Purpose: account and system security, preventing misuse. These records are kept for short periods set by the provider and then deleted automatically. Legal ground: legitimate interest (Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • The dates of your statement that you have read the Information Notice and of your explicit consent. Purpose: to be able to show that the obligation to inform has been fulfilled and that your explicit consent was obtained. Legal ground: fulfilment of a legal obligation (Art. 5(2)(ç)).
  • Location: used only on your phone to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction; it is not sent to the server and is not processed by us.

For your special categories of personal data, the adequate measures determined by the Board are taken: all connections are encrypted; in the database everyone can see only their own record and the shared view of their family circle (kaza prayer times are visible only to you); access rights are limited; these data are not used for advertising, marketing or profiling.

Without signing in (browsing mode) the app is for viewing only: nothing is marked or saved and no personal data is sent to the server. If an error occurs, you may choose to send only an error report that is not linked to your account.

3. Method of collection

Your personal data are collected by automated means in electronic form, through the app and its infrastructure providers, when you sign in with Google and as you use the app.

4. Transfers

Your personal data are not transferred to third parties within Türkiye. Information may be provided to public authorities only where required by law (Art. 5(2)(ç)).

Because the app's servers are outside Türkiye, the data above are transferred abroad within the scope of Article 9 of the Law: (a) Supabase Pte. Ltd. — database and sign-in infrastructure (data processor); data are stored in the European Union, on servers in Germany (Frankfurt). (b) Google LLC (Firebase Cloud Messaging and Google Sign-In) — delivering notifications and signing in; Google's servers abroad.

These transfers are made within the scope of Article 9 of the Law. You can request detailed information on the safeguard the transfers rely on and on the recipients through the application channel in section 7.

5. Retention periods

Account data and your records: until your account is deleted; family circle activity (duas, notification feed): 7 days; error reports: 30 days; the notification ID is deleted when you uninstall the app or the ID becomes invalid. When you delete your account, your data are deleted immediately.

6. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the Law, by applying to the data controller you have the right to:

  • a) learn whether your personal data are processed
  • b) request information if they have been processed
  • c) learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data are used in line with that purpose
  • ç) know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom the data are transferred
  • d) request rectification if the data are incomplete or inaccurate
  • e) request erasure or destruction under the conditions of Article 7 of the Law
  • f) request that the operations under (d) and (e) be notified to the third parties to whom the data were transferred
  • g) object to a result against you arising from analysis exclusively by automated systems
  • ğ) claim compensation if you suffer damage due to unlawful processing

7. Applications

You can send your requests in writing, or from the email address registered in the app to e.bilkil5391@gmail.com, in accordance with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles for Applications to the Data Controller. Your request will be concluded free of charge within 30 days at the latest, depending on its nature. You can also delete your account and all your data immediately via Settings → Delete my account and data. You can withdraw your explicit consent at any time: Settings → Delete my account and data deletes all your data, including your prayer and kaza records. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before.

8. Explicit Consent Text

I have read the Information Notice. I understand that the prayer marks and kaza counts I keep in the Aile İle Namaz app, and the dua and greeting messages I send to my family, are special categories of personal data that may reveal my religious belief. I give my explicit consent for these data to be processed so that the app can provide its prayer tracking and family circle service, and to be shown to the people in the family circles I join. I understand that these data are stored on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). I understand that I can withdraw my consent at any time by deleting my account and data.

Sharing

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising or marketing. Data are processed only on the infrastructure providers listed above, so that the app can work.

Service continuity

Features that need the internet, such as the family circle, family notifications and cloud backup, are available as long as the server infrastructure (Supabase, Google Firebase) is running; they may be temporarily unavailable during maintenance or outages. Prayer times and marks saved on your phone work offline and sync automatically when the connection returns.

Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). The database has row-level security rules: everyone can see only their own data and the data of the family circle they belong to.

Account deletion

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion of your account by following the steps on the Account deletion request page.

Children

The app is intended for use within the family. Accounts for children under 13 should be created under parental supervision.

Changes

When this notice is updated, the new date is published on this page; for significant changes you will also be informed in the app.
